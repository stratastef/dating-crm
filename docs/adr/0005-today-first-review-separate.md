# The app opens on Today, with Review as its own tab

v1 opened on the Pipeline, a flat list of cards by stage that hid everything needing the user. The redesign opens on **Today**: the user's to-dos by time (Overdue, Today, Coming up), with booked dates, due next actions, Stalled cards and dates to rate. AI items (drafts, handovers, paid items) stay in a separate **Review** tab with a count badge, and Today shows them only as one summary row, so no item appears twice. The tab bar has five tabs: Today, Review, Pipeline, Swipe, You. Dashboard, profile, preferences, connected apps and settings live under You.

## Considered options

Mocked up and rejected: a single ranked "Needs you" list that folds the review queue in; an email-style inbox; a list of every conversation with how the agent handles each. The owner chose a time-ordered first screen and kept AI review as its own batch task, matching the spec's "clear the queue 2–3 times a day".
